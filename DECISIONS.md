# DECISIONS: Receipts
> _Location: Projects / Personal / App Development / Receipts_

## Broader task
Build and maintain Receipts, Julian's personal receipt tracker that replaces the paper. Modelled loosely on Smart Receipts, but only the parts he uses.

## Decisions
- **Working preferences.** No em dashes, ever (the build refuses them). Australian English. AUD and day-first dates. Concise, with pushback.
- **2026-09-05, phone web app (PWA), not native.** Same pattern as Macro and Movement Tracker: one source file, local build, static hosting.
- **2026-09-05, one category plus any number of tags.** Categories are the main bucket (Electronics, Bills, plus any he adds; Warranty is a tag, not a category). Sections are tags, so a receipt can sit in both Warranty and Reimbursable. Julian chose this over a folder model.
- **2026-09-05, Claude reads the receipt, not on-device OCR.** `claude-opus-5`, effort low, JSON schema output. The model marks each of merchant, date, total and category high or low confidence. Anything low or missing is highlighted in the form and the receipt carries a "Check" chip until he saves it with the field edited.
- **2026-09-05, the crop is auto-detected and then hand-adjustable.** Corners and whole edges drag, and on release they snap to the strongest detected straight line nearby. The detector scores every combination of candidate lines and keeps the most plausible quadrilateral: near right angles, opposite sides parallel, and it must contain the printed text. Edges come from colour and saturation as well as brightness, plus a coarse-scale pass that keeps paper-on-white-desk steps while text washes out. A side that cannot be seen is guessed just beyond the text, parallel to its opposite. "Auto" restores the detection, "Full" resets to the whole frame. Every image view pinch-zooms.
- **2026-09-05, stills come from ImageCapture at full sensor resolution**, falling back to the video frame. Small print on receipts is the reason. Stored pages are capped at 2800 px on the long side; Claude gets a 1568 px copy because the API downsizes anyway.
- **2026-09-05, a saved receipt can be re-read.** "Read again with Claude" on the detail screen re-extracts from the stored pages and opens the form with the new values, existing comments and tags kept.
- **2026-09-05, enhanced scans are the default.** Divide-by-local-mean greyscale, so paper goes white and faint ink stays readable. Colour is one tap away per page, and the default is a setting.
- **2026-09-05, camera is in-page getUserMedia, never the system camera.** Carried over from Macro: Android kills the backgrounded tab when the camera app opens. Lens switcher remembers his choice.
- **2026-09-06, Drive sign-in works like Macro's: one Connect button, client ID compiled in.** Same OAuth client as Macro, because both apps share the `jules1342.github.io` origin and Google authorises by origin. Nothing to paste. Restore sits behind a two-tap confirm. Untested until the first run on the phone.
- **2026-09-05, GitHub Pages is the host, not Netlify.** Julian's call: a fixed URL (`https://jules1342.github.io/receipts/`) and a deploy Claude can run end to end with a push. Google only allows Drive sign-in from a registered origin, so the URL must never change.
- **Export is a zip with the images and a CSV**, shared through the phone's share sheet so it can go straight to Drive without any Google setup. Import merges by id and keeps the newer copy.
- **2026-09-06, duplicates are flagged, never auto-removed.** Same date and total, same merchant and total a day apart, or same date and merchant with one total missing. Checked on every save and on demand in Settings. Each pair offers merge (keep wins, blanks fill, pages and tags union), delete one, or keep both, and a keep-both answer is remembered.
- **2026-09-06, every sheet is a history entry.** The phone's Back closes the top sheet instead of the app. Code never fights the browser history: a sheet closed by a button consumes its own entry only when it is on top, and pushes wait while a back is in flight. Search text and list scroll survive opening an item.
- **2026-09-06, receipt numbers are read and used for duplicates.** Claude extracts the printed receipt or invoice number; a matching number is a duplicate signal on its own. Bulk re-read applies Claude's confident values and flags the rest, from a multi-select on the list.
- **2026-09-06, the white screen was the duplicate page rendering nothing.** After a merge or delete the parent recomputed the pair list underneath the page, and an index past the end rendered null. Review screens now snapshot their list when they open, skip pairs whose receipts are gone, and close themselves rather than render nothing.
- **2026-09-06, a white screen is a caught error, never a blank.** An error boundary shows the message with a Reload button, and the last errors sit in Diagnostics. Capture images are capped at 3200 px and pixel work uses CPU canvases, to keep memory under the phone's limit.
- **2026-09-09, the "ledger" restyle lives beside the live app, not in it.** Julian asked to compare before choosing, and found the neutral house default too close to the original. The copy at `/receipts/design/` keeps every screen and all logic, generated from `receipts.html` by `_build/make-design.py`, and applies a chosen personality: paper surfaces, a serif for titles and amounts, hairline rows, a month summary, one ink-green accent, 12/14/16/20/24 type, two weights, 4/8 spacing, radius 6/8, borders not shadows, light and dark with a setting, bound labels, alt text, single-column forms, toasts not alerts. Both builds share the phone's data; `?demo` opens either on an isolated sample database.
